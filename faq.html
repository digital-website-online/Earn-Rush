EarnRush Help Center

Frequently Asked Questions

Find clear answers about EarnRush gameplay, Coins, XP, levels, missions, accounts and available platform features.

What is EarnRush?

EarnRush is a browser-based gaming platform focused on simple gameplay, progression, missions and account features.

How do I start playing?

Open the EarnRush home page and use the available gameplay controls. Your dashboard shows the main information you need while playing.

What are Coins?

Coins are the main in-game currency displayed in EarnRush. Your balance can change according to eligible gameplay and the current game rules.

What is XP?

XP is used for player progression. Eligible gameplay activities can contribute XP toward the next level.

What are levels?

Levels represent your progression in EarnRush. Your current level and XP progress are shown on the main dashboard.

What are missions?

Missions are additional objectives available in the EarnRush Missions section. Each mission can have its own requirements and reward information.

How do rewards work?

Rewards depend on the specific feature or mission. When a reward is available, EarnRush displays the relevant requirements and claim information inside the platform.

Do I need an account?

Some EarnRush features can require an authenticated account. If a feature requires sign-in, the platform will show the relevant account prompt.

How does the streak system work?

The streak system tracks consecutive activity according to the current EarnRush game rules. Your current streak can be displayed in the player status area.

Where can I check my progress?

Your main dashboard provides information such as your Coin balance, level, XP, combo, streak and other available game progress.

Is withdrawal always available?

Withdrawal availability depends on the current platform rules, account eligibility, available balance and system status. Always check the Withdrawal section for the current requirements.

Where can I see withdrawal requirements?

The current Withdrawal section displays the applicable minimum requirement, supported payment options and the information needed before submitting a request.

Can EarnRush features change?

Yes. EarnRush is an actively developed platform, so features, requirements, availability and interface details may change in future updates.

What should I do if something does not work?

First refresh the page and make sure you are using a current browser. If an account or platform issue continues, use the available support or contact option.

Need help?

If your question is not answered here, check the information shown on the relevant EarnRush screen first. Current in-app information takes priority because features and requirements can change with updates.

EarnRush Guide

How EarnRush works

Learn the basic gameplay, progression system, Coins, XP, missions and account features available on EarnRush.

Getting started with EarnRush

EarnRush is designed around a simple progression experience. You can start from the main dashboard and use the available game features to build your in-game progress.

1

Open the game

Visit the EarnRush home screen to see your current Coins, XP, level, streak and available activities.

2

Play and interact

Use the available gameplay controls and follow the instructions shown inside the game.

3

Build your progress

Gameplay activity can increase your in-game Coins and XP and contribute to your overall progression.

4

Check missions and rewards

Review the missions and reward features available to your account and complete eligible objectives.

Understanding Coins

Coins are the main in-game currency displayed throughout EarnRush. Your current balance can be viewed from the dashboard.

🪙

Coin Balance

Your balance shows the Coins currently associated with your EarnRush game state or account.

🎯

Gameplay Progress

Eligible gameplay actions can affect your Coin balance according to the current game rules.

XP and Levels

XP represents your progression through the EarnRush level system. As your XP increases, the game can move you toward the next level.

⭐

Earn XP

Eligible gameplay activities can award XP according to the current game logic.

📊

Level Progress

Your current level and XP progress are displayed on the main game interface.

Daily activity and streaks

EarnRush includes a streak system that can track consecutive activity. Your current streak is displayed in the player status area when available.

Important: Streaks and other progression features follow the rules implemented in the current version of the platform and may change as EarnRush is updated.

Missions and rewards

Missions give players additional objectives to work toward. The Missions area can display available tasks and their corresponding progress or rewards.

Reward availability and requirements depend on the specific mission. Always review the current information shown inside EarnRush before claiming a reward.

📋

Available Missions

Check the Missions section to see which objectives are currently available.

🎁

Claimable Rewards

When a mission is eligible for a reward, follow the instructions displayed by the platform.

Account and settings

Creating or signing in to an EarnRush account provides access to the account-related features available on the platform.

The Settings area can be used to manage supported preferences and account options. Available controls may change as new versions are released.

Withdrawal information

Where withdrawal functionality is available, eligible users can use the Withdrawal section from the EarnRush dashboard.

The platform displays the applicable minimum requirement, available payment methods and account information fields before a withdrawal request is submitted.

Withdrawal requests are subject to the current EarnRush rules, account eligibility, available balance and system processing.

Keep in mind: The information displayed inside the live EarnRush dashboard is the current source for applicable requirements and availability.

Use EarnRush fairly

Use your own account and interact with the platform as intended. Attempts to manipulate game data, exploit technical issues or interfere with other users may result in account restrictions.

Never rely on outdated information when a current requirement is shown inside the application. Always check the relevant screen before completing an account action.

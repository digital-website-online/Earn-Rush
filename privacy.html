About this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains the types of information that may be handled when you use EarnRush and how that information is used to operate, maintain and improve the service.

By using EarnRush, you should review this policy together with the EarnRush Terms & Conditions.

1. Information We May Handle

Depending on how you use EarnRush, the service may handle information needed to provide account, gameplay and reward-related features.

Account information

If you create an account, EarnRush may handle information associated with your account, such as your name and email address.

Gameplay and progress information

EarnRush may store information related to your game progress, including Coins, XP, levels, missions, rewards, streaks and other gameplay state needed to provide the service.

Withdrawal information

If you request a withdrawal, information required to process the selected withdrawal method may be submitted through the withdrawal feature. Depending on the selected method, this can include the relevant mobile number or bank account information entered by you.

Technical and usage information

Standard technical and usage information may be processed by EarnRush and the third-party services used to operate analytics, advertising and the application infrastructure.

2. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

  • Provide and maintain EarnRush features.
  • Manage user accounts and authentication.
  • Save and restore gameplay progress.
  • Provide missions, rewards and other game features.
  • Process and review withdrawal requests.
  • Detect and investigate suspicious or abusive activity.
  • Maintain security and protect the service.
  • Understand service usage and improve EarnRush.
  • Display and measure advertising where advertising is enabled.
  • Respond to support or other user communications.

3. Local Storage and Similar Technologies

EarnRush uses browser storage for certain game and user-interface functions. This can include local storage used to preserve gameplay state and other settings or identifiers required by the application.

Browser storage is controlled by your browser and can normally be cleared through your browser settings. Clearing stored information may affect locally saved gameplay state or other application preferences.

4. Google Analytics

EarnRush uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the website and to help improve the service.

Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies and may process information about website usage and device or browser activity.

Your browser may provide controls for managing cookies and related storage. Google also provides information and controls regarding its analytics services through its own policies and settings.

5. Advertising

EarnRush may display advertising through third-party advertising services. These services may use cookies, identifiers or similar technologies to provide, measure or personalize advertising, depending on the provider and applicable settings.

EarnRush does not control the privacy practices of independent third-party advertising providers. Users should review the privacy information provided by those services where appropriate.

6. Supabase and Application Infrastructure

EarnRush uses Supabase as part of its application infrastructure. Account and application data may therefore be processed or stored through services used to operate the EarnRush backend.

Access to application data is intended to be limited through the application's authentication and database security controls.

7. Information Sharing

EarnRush does not intend to sell users' personal information as a normal part of operating the service.

Information may be processed by service providers that help operate the website, authentication, database infrastructure, analytics, advertising or other required services.

Information may also be disclosed where reasonably necessary to protect the service, investigate abuse or fraud, comply with legal obligations, or protect the rights and safety of users and the platform.

8. Security and Fraud Prevention

EarnRush is designed to use account, authentication and application controls to help protect the service from unauthorized or abusive activity.

Users must not attempt to manipulate Coins, rewards, referrals, withdrawals, accounts or other platform systems through bots, automation, exploits, fake accounts or other abusive methods.

No internet service can guarantee absolute security, but reasonable measures are used to protect information and maintain the integrity of the platform.

9. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain account and transaction records, protect against abuse, resolve disputes, comply with applicable obligations, and maintain legitimate business and security needs.

Locally stored browser data may remain until it is cleared by the user or otherwise removed by the application.

10. Your Choices

You can control certain browser storage and cookie settings through your browser. Some settings may affect how parts of EarnRush work.

If you have a question about information associated with your EarnRush account or need assistance, you can contact EarnRush through the available support/contact method.

11. Children

EarnRush is intended to be used by people who are legally permitted to use the service under applicable laws and the EarnRush Terms & Conditions.

Users should not provide information that they are not legally permitted to provide.

12. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when EarnRush changes its features, services, technology or legal requirements.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page with the applicable effective date or updated information.

13. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how information is handled by EarnRush, please use the available EarnRush contact or support channel.

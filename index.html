EARNRUSH GUIDE

What is EarnRush?

EarnRush is a browser-based game focused on simple, interactive gameplay and player progression. Tap the reactor, collect Coins, gain XP, complete available missions, and work toward your in-game milestones.

⚡

Tap & Progress

Use the Tap-Tap reactor to play and build your in-game Coin balance while progressing through the available game levels.

⭐

Coins & XP

Coins are the main in-game balance, while XP contributes to your level progression and helps track your progress.

🎯

Missions

Check the Missions section for available tasks and progress opportunities that are supported by the current game.

🎁

Rewards

Rewards are designed around game milestones and progression. Available rewards and requirements are shown inside EarnRush.

🔥

Daily Progress

Your streak and level provide additional ways to follow your progress as you continue using the game.

👤

Your Account

Creating an account allows supported account features to be used, including access to your withdrawal history.

How EarnRush Works

Start by playing the main Tap-Tap game. Your Coins, XP, level, combo and streak are displayed on the main screen. Missions and Rewards provide additional progression areas, while account and settings controls are available from the header. For a detailed explanation of the available features, visit the How It Works guide.
